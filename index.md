I am a software engineer specializing in language runtimes, garbage collection, and performance engineering. I enjoy building systems that turn research ideas into reliable, high-performance software.

I am the lead developer of [mmtk-core](https://github.com/mmtk/mmtk-core), the core of [MMTk](https://mmtk.io) (Memory Management ToolKit), a framework for reusable garbage collectors across language runtimes. My work has helped take MMTk from a research prototype to a mature open-source project, spanning its architecture, collector implementations, performance analysis, and automated correctness and performance testing. I am also a [Julia](https://github.com/JuliaLang/julia) committer. I led the integration of MMTk into Julia, working with the Julia community to introduce generational and concurrent garbage collection and develop support for moving collectors.

I worked on MMTk at the Australian National University from 2020 to 2026. Earlier, I built backend services for IoT fleet management and geospatial data processing at TracMap. I earned my PhD in Computer Science at ANU, where I was a core contributor to the [micro virtual machines](https://microvm.org) project and wrote most of [Zebu VM](https://gitlab.anu.edu.au/mu/mu-impl-fast/). My thesis is [An Efficient Implementation of a Micro Virtual Machine](./assets/yilin_phd_thesis.pdf). More details about my work and publications are in my [CV](./assets/yilin_cv.pdf).

I enjoy the creative side of programming. One of my favorite quotes is Donald Knuth's: "a programmer who subconsciously views himself as an artist will enjoy what he does and will do it better." Outside programming, I enjoy video games, board games, and playing the violin, which has become a lifelong hobby.
